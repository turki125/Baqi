---
name: Thmanyah publishing permission
description: Why public webfont deployment needs a rights check before publishing Baqi.
---

Confirm that the supplied Thmanyah license or separate permission covers ordinary
browser webfont delivery before publishing these fonts.

**Why:** The supplied license permits website/application embedding in a compiled,
packaged, or obfuscated product, but also restricts independently downloadable or
extractable font files. Standard browser WOFF2 delivery remains extractable even
when a build fingerprints filenames. The user's upload is not evidence that this
specific ambiguity has been resolved.

**How to apply:** Preserve the supplied license and font metadata. Do not claim
that bundling or hashed URLs establish redistribution permission. Explain the
ambiguity and seek confirmation before a public release involving these fonts.
